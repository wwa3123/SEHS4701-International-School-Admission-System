Campus

Facilities and laboratories

Purpose-built spaces for science, design, computing and the arts — all on one campus, all used by every year group from S1 to S6.

See the programmes Visit the campus

Science laboratories

Three dedicated laboratories support the MYP sciences and the DP science courses at both Standard and Higher Level.

Laboratory

Physics laboratory

Bench power supplies, data loggers, optics benches and a demonstration area. Capacity 28 students.

Laboratory

Chemistry laboratory

Fume hoods, balances and a dedicated preparation room, with separate storage for hazardous chemicals. Capacity 28 students.

Laboratory

Biology laboratory

Microscopy stations, incubators and a freshwater aquarium for ecology practicals. Capacity 28 students.

Design, technology and computing

MYP design and the DP sciences both depend on making things; these rooms are booked for timetabled classes and after-school projects.

Workshop

Design & technology workshop

Hand tools, laser cutter, 3D printers and a supervised soldering bench for prototyping.

Computing

Two computer laboratories

48 workstations across two rooms, used for computer science, digital design and cross-curricular research.

Media

Media and recording studio

Soundproofed recording booth, editing suites and a small green-screen area for film and podcast projects.

Library, arts and wellbeing

Spaces that support independent study, creative work and the DP core.

Study

Library and media centre

12,000 titles in English and Chinese, silent study zones, group tables and a dedicated DP coursework collection.

Arts

Music rooms and performance hall

Four practice rooms, a black-box drama studio and a 320-seat hall for concerts, assemblies and the DP CAS showcase.

Sport

Sports hall and outdoor courts

Full-size indoor hall, fitness room and covered outdoor courts for basketball and badminton.

Wellbeing

Student commons and counselling suite

A supervised common room, quiet room and two private counselling rooms staffed by the pastoral team.

Dining

Canteen and courtyard

Hot lunch service with vegetarian options, plus a covered courtyard used for breaks and club activities.

Access

Accessibility

Lift access to all floors, accessible washrooms and step-free routes between buildings. Contact admissions to discuss specific needs.

Come and see the campus

Campus visits run during term time. Contact the admissions team to arrange a time.
