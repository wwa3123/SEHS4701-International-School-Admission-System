Admission Requirements

General requirements for admission to each programme level.

Applicants should have completed primary education. Basic English proficiency is expected. Previous school reports may be required.

Applicants should have completed the previous year level. School reports and a recommendation letter may be requested. English language ability appropriate to the grade level is required.

Successful completion of MYP Year 5 (or equivalent). Strong academic performance and English proficiency are expected. Subject prerequisites may apply for certain Higher Level subjects.

S6 is normally reserved for continuing students from S5. Admission to S6 for new students is only possible when the Super Administrator opens S6 for that admission year.
