How the application works

Five steps, all completed through this website. No entrance test or interview is required.

  1. Create a member account Register with a parent or guardian email address and verify the one-time code sent to it.
  2. Add your applicant Record the student's identity document and personal details under your membership.
  3. Submit one application Choose a single programme level inside its application window and declare that the information is true.
  4. Wait for the result An admissions administrator reviews every application; results are released after the closing date.
  5. Respond to an offer If an offer is made, accept or reject it through the website within seven days.

Requirements by level

The table below sets out the academic expectation for each level. Meeting a requirement does not guarantee a seat; offers depend on the number of places available in that programme level.

Academic requirements for entry
Level Programme Academic requirement Language requirement
S1 IB MYP Completion of primary education (Primary 6 or equivalent). Evidence of English proficiency appropriate to age.
S2 – S4 IB MYP Completion of the previous year level, supported by school reports. English level appropriate to the year group.
S5 IB DP Successful completion of MYP year 4 or equivalent, with strong results in intended HL subjects. English proficiency sufficient for DP coursework.
S6 IB DP Normally reserved for students continuing from S5 at this school. As for S5.

S6 opens to new applicants only when the school opens S6 for that admission year. While S6 is closed, its seat quota is held for students continuing from S5.

Documents to prepare

Have these ready before you start; scanned copies or clear photographs are accepted.

  • The applicant's HKID or passport (either document is accepted)
  • Two recent school reports, or a transcript for the last two years
  • Evidence of English language proficiency, if available
  • A recent passport-style photograph
  • Recommendation letter from the current school (optional)
  • Proof of address for the family (optional)

Identity documents are checked for duplicates. An HKID or passport may be linked to one membership only, and the email address used to register cannot be changed later.

Assessment Applications are assessed by an admissions administrator on the documents you submit. There is no entrance test or interview.
One application at a time An applicant may hold one application in an admission year; a new one is possible only after the previous application is cancelled.
Offer response An offer must be accepted or rejected through the website within seven days of release.
