Start an application Programme introduction

How the application works

Four steps, all completed through this website.

  1. Create a member account Register with a parent or guardian email address and verify the one-time code sent to it.
  2. Submit one application Choose an open programme level, enter the applicant's details and accept the declarations.
  3. Wait for the result An admissions administrator reviews every application; results are released after the closing date.
  4. Respond to an offer Log in to accept or reject an offer within 168 hours of release. Confirm your choice before saving; it is final. An unanswered offer becomes Rejected when its deadline expires, even if the email was not delivered.

Requirements by level

These are the application rules for each programme. They do not specify minimum grades or English-test scores. Submitting an application does not guarantee an offer; admission depends on the administrator's assessment and the available seats.

Programme entry and application requirements
Level Programme Application requirement
S1 – S4 IB MYP A registered parent or guardian must log in and apply for one level during its application window in the open admission year.
S5 IB DP The same membership, application-window and declaration requirements apply to entry to the Diploma Programme.
S6 IB DP Closed to new applicants by default; its quota is reserved for continuing S5 students. A new application is possible only if the Super Administrator opens S6 and its application window is current.

Each level has its own dates and quota. Application dates are inclusive and use Hong Kong time.

Information to prepare

The application asks for the applicant's personal details and identity document number.

  • Full name, date of birth, gender and phone number
  • Identity document type and number, and issuing country when required
  • Previous school, if applicable

Document files and photographs cannot be uploaded through this website.

Identity documents are checked for duplicates. An HKID or passport may be linked to one membership only, and the email address used to register cannot be changed later.

Assessment An admissions administrator reviews the information provided in the application. There is no entrance test or interview.
One application at a time An applicant may hold one application in an admission year; a new one is possible only after the previous application is cancelled.
