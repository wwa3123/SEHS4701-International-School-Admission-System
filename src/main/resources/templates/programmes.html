Programmes

One school, two IB programmes

Students join us in the Middle Years Programme from S1 to S4, then progress into the Diploma Programme for S5 and S6. The two programmes are designed to connect: the skills built in MYP are the foundation for DP success.

The IB continuum at CIS

Both programmes are authorised by the International Baccalaureate and taught by subject specialists. Assessment is criterion-referenced: students are judged against published IB standards, not against each other.

S1 – S4

IB Middle Years Programme (MYP)

The MYP is a five-year framework, of which we deliver years 1 to 4 across S1 to S4. Students study eight subject groups every year, so choices stay open until the Diploma Programme.

Learning is organised around key concepts and global contexts. In each unit students inquire, act and reflect; in the final MYP year they complete a personal project assessed against IB criteria.

  • Eight subject groups, taught by specialist teachers
  • Criterion-referenced assessment reported to parents each term
  • Personal project in the final MYP year
  • Service learning embedded in the curriculum
S5 – S6

IB Diploma Programme (DP)

The DP is a two-year programme for S5 and S6. Students choose six subjects — three at Higher Level and three at Standard Level — and complete the DP core.

The core — Theory of Knowledge, the Extended Essay and Creativity, Activity, Service — is what distinguishes the DP from a collection of A-Level-style courses, and it is where universities look for evidence of independent work.

  • Six subjects: three at Higher Level, three at Standard Level
  • Theory of Knowledge, Extended Essay and CAS
  • External IB examinations at the end of S6
  • University guidance from the DP coordinator

Which programme applies to which level?

The mapping is fixed: S1 to S4 follow the IB MYP, and S5 to S6 follow the IB DP. The same mapping is shown when an application is completed.
