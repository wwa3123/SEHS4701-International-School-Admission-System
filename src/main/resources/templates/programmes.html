Programme Introduction

Christian International School offers the International Baccalaureate continuum.

IB Middle Years Programme (MYP)

Levels: S1 – S4

The MYP is a challenging framework that encourages students to make practical connections between their studies and the real world. It develops critical thinking, intercultural understanding and strong academic skills.

IB Diploma Programme (DP)

Levels: S5 – S6

The DP is a rigorous two-year programme that prepares students for university and adult life. Students study six subject groups together with the DP Core (Theory of Knowledge, Extended Essay and CAS).

Note: S1 to S4 follow the IB MYP, and S5 to S6 follow the IB DP (as stated in the business rules).
