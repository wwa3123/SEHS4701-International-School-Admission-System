How to apply

Five steps, all completed on this website. There is no entrance test or interview - an admissions administrator assesses the documents you submit.

  1. Create a member account Register with a parent or guardian email address and verify the one-time code sent to it.
  2. Add your applicant Record the student's personal details and identity document under your membership.
  3. Submit one application Choose a single programme level inside its application window and sign the declaration.
  4. Wait for the result Every application is reviewed after the closing date; results are released through the website.
  5. Respond to an offer If an offer is made, accept or reject it within seven days of release.
