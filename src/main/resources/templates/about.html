Our story

Founded in 1998, Christian International School has grown from a small secondary school into a full IB continuum school serving families across Hong Kong. Our approach combines academic rigour with pastoral care: every student is known by name, and every family has a named point of contact.

Learning is inquiry-based. Students are expected to ask questions, gather evidence and defend conclusions - skills that carry directly into the IB Middle Years Programme and the Diploma Programme.

We are a community of approximately 900 students and 80 teaching staff, supported by specialist laboratories, studios, a media library and a sports hall on a single campus.
