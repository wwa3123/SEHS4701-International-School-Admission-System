S1 – S4

IB MYP course groups

Every MYP student studies all eight subject groups each year. The emphasis shifts from breadth in S1 towards deeper disciplinary work by S4.

MYP subject groups, S1 to S4
Subject group What students learn Notes
Language and literature Close reading, analytical and creative writing in the student's strongest language. English or Chinese
Language acquisition An additional language, from beginner to advanced phase. Chinese, French or Spanish
Individuals and societies History, geography and the social sciences through inquiry into people and places. Integrated in S1–S3
Sciences Integrated biology, chemistry and physics with practical work in the laboratories. Three laboratory rooms
Mathematics Number, algebra, geometry and statistics, with extended and standard routes. Set by readiness
Arts Visual art, music and drama, rotating across the year. Studio and performance spaces
Physical and health education Movement, team sport, fitness and health literacy. Sports hall and courts
Design Product, digital and systems design, taught in the workshop and computer labs. Personal project in the final year
S5 – S6

IB DP subjects and core

DP students choose one subject from each of the six groups, with three taken at Higher Level and three at Standard Level. The core runs alongside.

DP subject groups, S5 and S6
Group Courses offered Levels
1. Studies in language and literature English A: Literature; Chinese A: Literature HL / SL
2. Language acquisition English B; Chinese B; French ab initio HL / SL
3. Individuals and societies History; Economics; Psychology HL / SL
4. Sciences Biology; Chemistry; Physics; Computer Science HL / SL
5. Mathematics Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches; Applications and Interpretation HL / SL
6. The arts (or a second subject from groups 2–4) Visual Arts; Music HL / SL
