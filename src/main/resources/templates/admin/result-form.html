Set or correct result

Application:

Administrator use only. Set an initial result after this programme level's closing date. Saving releases the result immediately and queues its email.

Admissions Officers and Super Administrators may change Pending to Success, Wait or Fail. A Wait application may become Success only when a seat is available; the administrator chooses who receives it.

Changing a released result is a correction: only a Super Administrator may do this before the member responds. Enter a reason; the previous result is kept and a corrected-result email is sent.

Leave blank for a first result. Corrections require a reason and are limited to unresponded offers.

No result changes are available for this application.
